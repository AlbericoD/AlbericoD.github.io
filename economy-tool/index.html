About Economy Tool.

Is this a calculator I can use in my browser?

No. Economy Tool is a Windows app distributed through Overwolf. Its economy overlay works with CS2 match context; this website explains it and links to the official installer.

What does safe to drop mean?

It is the amount the app indicates a player can spend on drops after accounting for the configured buy needs and reserve. Read it together with the team buy call and the current round.

Does it know the exact enemy balance?

No. Enemy economy is an estimate based on available round evidence. It should not be read as an exact view of opponents’ money.

Can I adjust the reserve buffer?

Yes. The reserve buffer lets you retain money in the calculation instead of treating all available cash as spendable on drops.
