What it does

A decision-support layer, not just a stats dashboard.

DeepRivals connects live Marvel Rivals game events with local match history and focused review surfaces. The goal is to keep active-match information glanceable while preserving enough evidence after the match to understand hero choices, team context, objective flow, and personal performance.

Core features

Context during the match. Evidence after it.

Hero swap insights

Recommendations and context based on the match state that DeepRivals can observe.

Live overlays

Configurable surfaces for recommendations, recent switches, ultimate status, team alive, damage recap, KDA, performance, and rank/Elo session context.

Matchups

Review performance with specific heroes against specific enemies, with map and game-mode context.

Match history and session review

Keep recent match evidence connected to heroes played, KDA, damage, block, healing, maps, and other available facts.

Contextual highlights

Connect captured moments with the match context that produced them.

Post-game tools

Richer match review, exports, and latest-match analysis after the pressure of active play is gone.

Who it is for

For players who want more than a profile tracker.

DeepRivals is aimed at Marvel Rivals players who want more than a profile tracker. It is especially useful for players who want help understanding when a hero switch may be worth considering, how fights and team states changed, and what their own match history says over time.

Different by design

Live context first, then review.

Traditional trackers often focus on profiles, ranks, aggregate stats, and leaderboards. DeepRivals is centered on the continuity between the current match and the review that follows it: live context first, then history and evidence that can be inspected later.

Frequently asked questions

Common questions.

DeepRivals is built to support a player's decisions with the context available to the app, not to replace the player's judgment.

What is DeepRivals?

DeepRivals is a Marvel Rivals decision-support companion for Windows and Overwolf. It combines live overlays, hero-switch context, matchup analysis, match history, contextual highlights, and post-game review.

Does DeepRivals recommend hero switches?

Yes. It can surface recommendations using the live match context, hero swaps, team state, and performance evidence available to the app. It is designed to support a player's decision rather than replace it.

Does it work during a match?

Yes. DeepRivals includes configurable in-game overlays and companion views intended to stay compact and glanceable while you play.

Where is the official download?
