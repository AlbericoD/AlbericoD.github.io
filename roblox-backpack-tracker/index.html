About Roblox Backpack Tracker.

Does it automatically sync every Roblox inventory?

No. Quick Add and manual organization are the dependable baseline. Do not assume an Experience has automatic tracking just because it appears in the app.

Are My Values official market prices?

No. My Values holds your personal price notes. Backpack totals reflect those notes and your recorded items; they are not verified market prices or trade offers.

Does it support multiple accounts?

Yes. You can organize Backpacks by account and use All Accounts for a combined view.

Can I use it across Roblox Experiences?

Yes. You can start Backpacks for different Experiences and record items manually. A dedicated automatic integration is not required for manual organization.
