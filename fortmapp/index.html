What it does

Understand where your Fortnite matches happened.

Fortmapp is built around spatial context. Instead of reducing a match to a list of numbers, it connects match events and player movement back to the Fortnite map so rotations, locations, and key moments are easier to review.

Who it is for

For players who want to review rotations, not only stats.

Fortmapp is useful for Fortnite players who want to review rotations, compare locations, and understand the relationship between movement and match outcomes. It is especially relevant when the question is not only “what happened?” but “where did it happen and how did I get there?”

Map-first analysis

Location itself is evidence.

The product's distinguishing idea is that location itself is evidence. Route tracking and map replay make spatial decisions visible, which is difficult to infer from ordinary stat tables alone.

Frequently asked questions

Common questions.

Fortmapp is centered on spatial context: where the match happened, how you moved, and what those locations meant.

What is Fortmapp?

Fortmapp is a Windows and Overwolf companion for Fortnite that combines an interactive map, location-based match stats, route tracking, map-based replays, and match analysis prompts.

Can I review my route after a match?

Yes. Fortmapp can replay recorded movement and key match moments on the map so rotations can be inspected in spatial context.

Does it track routes live?

Yes. Live route tracking is one of the core features, allowing the map to reflect your movement during the match.

Can I use the match data with an LLM?

Yes. Fortmapp can generate structured match prompts intended for review in tools such as ChatGPT or Gemini.
