What it does

A Backpack notebook that stays useful while you play.

The desktop app acts as the full Backpack editor. In-game, the app stays intentionally small: a compact estimated-value pill plus Quick Add for recording an item without turning the overlay into another dashboard.

Core features

Track, value, organize, and review.

Value history

Review trusted Backpack value observations over time without fabricating missing days.

Optional automatic ingestion

Supported Experiences can provide richer catalog or inventory data when that capability is available; manual Backpacks remain the base model.

Across Roblox

Designed to stay generic across Roblox.

The app does not require every Roblox Experience to have a dedicated integration. A detected Experience can remain transient until you use Quick Add, explicitly start a Backpack, add an item from the desktop app, or receive a valid supported automatic inventory update.

This keeps unsupported Experiences useful instead of forcing the product into a small fixed list of games.

One Backpack model

Manual and automatic tracking are one product.

There is no separate “manual notebook” versus “automatic notebook” concept. There is only a Backpack. Automatic tracking, where supported, is simply another way of adding or updating data inside the same Backpack model.

Frequently asked questions

Common questions.

The product stays intentionally generic: a Backpack works manually everywhere, with enhanced ingestion only where supported.

What is Roblox Backpack Tracker?

It is a Windows and Overwolf companion for organizing Backpack items, values, Roblox Experiences, accounts, and inventory history.

Does it support multiple Roblox accounts?

Yes. Backpacks are partitioned by account and Experience, while an All Accounts view can aggregate saved value when you want a combined picture.

Does it work with any Roblox Experience?

Any valid detected Experience can become a manual Backpack after meaningful interaction. Some Experiences may also support enhanced or automatic ingestion when that capability is available.

How do item values work?

You can maintain your own values through My Values. Supported Experience integrations may also provide catalog or valuation knowledge, but the product remains usable without that.

Where is the official download?
