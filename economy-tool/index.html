What it does

Make the economy call before freeze time ends.

Economy Tool is built around one job: help a CS2 player understand the current or latest economy state within a few seconds. It complements the game HUD rather than trying to replace it.

Core features

Fast calls without fake precision.

Team economy state

Surface Full Buy, Buy With Drops, Force Buy, Eco, or Mixed Buy based on the available match state.

Player-level roles

Identify players who can safely drop, are already covered for a buy, should save, or are in an eco state.

Safe-drop logic

Estimate how much a player can spend helping teammates while preserving the configured next-round reserve.

Side-aware buy costs

Account for the fact that CT and T full-buy costs differ.

Loss-bonus context

Incorporate round-economy context when estimating what the next round may look like.

Enemy economy estimation

Provide a readable enemy-economy state based on available loss-streak evidence instead of pretending to know exact money.

Adjustable risk profile

Let the user choose how conservative or aggressive the reserve buffer should be.

AI-ready prompts

Format current or saved match context for analysis in LLM tools such as ChatGPT or Gemini.

Why use it

Reduce the mental math during freeze time.

CS2 economy decisions are time-sensitive and team-dependent. The app keeps the logic consistent across rounds, makes drop responsibilities more visible, and reduces the amount of arithmetic the player has to do during freeze time.

Evidence over invention

Designed to avoid fake precision.

Enemy economy is presented as an estimate based on evidence the app can actually observe, not as invented exact balances. The same principle applies throughout the product: live and saved match evidence stays more important than decorative explanation.

Frequently asked questions

Common questions.

The app is deliberately conservative about what it claims to know, especially when estimating the enemy side.

What does Economy Tool do in CS2?

It turns available team money and round context into readable buy calls, player economy roles, safe-drop guidance, and a simple enemy-economy estimate.

Does it tell me who should drop?

Yes. Player-level logic can distinguish who can safely help, who already has enough for the intended buy, and who still needs help.

How does safe-drop logic work?

It considers current money, the expected cost of the next buy, loss-bonus context, and a configurable reserve buffer. The goal is to help a teammate without unnecessarily breaking your own next-round economy.

Does it know the enemy team's exact money?

No. The enemy side is treated as an estimate based on available round evidence. The app deliberately avoids presenting unsupported exact values.
