Frequently asked questions

Everything you need to know.

Quick answers about the apps, distribution, and the direct installer links on this site.

Where do I download the apps?

Each product page has a direct Windows download button that uses the official Overwolf installer. The canonical Overwolf Store listing is also linked on every app page.

Are the downloads distributed through Overwolf?

Yes. The direct links on this site point to Overwolf's installer endpoint, while the Store pages remain the canonical public product listings.

Which games are covered?

The current apps cover Marvel Rivals, Roblox, Counter-Strike 2, and Fortnite.

Why does this site exist if the apps already have Store pages?

It provides stable, crawlable reference pages for product capabilities, terminology, structured data, official links, and direct installer attribution.
