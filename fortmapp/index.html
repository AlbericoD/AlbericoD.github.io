About Fortmapp.

Does Fortmapp track routes during a match?

Yes. Fortmapp provides live route tracking on an interactive Fortnite map, including an in-game view and a second-screen workflow.

Is a map replay the same as a video replay?

No. A map-based replay is a way to review a match geographically. Do not assume it is a full video recording or Fortnite’s native replay file.

Can I compare how I play in different locations?

Fortmapp includes location-based match stats, so you can relate match results to places on the map.

Does the map always look like this screenshot?

The screenshot demonstrates the app’s map and route workflow. Fortnite maps change between seasons; the image is not a promise of the current island layout.
