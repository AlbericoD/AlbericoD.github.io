About

Built and maintained by one independent developer

I am Albérico Dias Barreto Filho, a software engineer and independent developer. These products are designed as practical game companions: they use game context and player-controlled data to make information easier to act on without replacing the game itself.

The Overwolf Store listings remain the canonical download destinations. This site exists as a stable, crawlable reference for product capabilities, supported games, terminology, and official links.
